## 3-minute pitch — BlockBid

1. **Problem (20s):** Ads are sold as slow packages. The eyeballs that matter are *right now* — one hero slot, many advertisers.
2. **Demo (90s):** Open live URL. Wallet A bids. Wallet B outbids (A refunded). Wait for blocks → Settle. Winner publishes headline on the billboard.
3. **Why Monad (20s):** Rebidding every few seconds only works when fees are dust and finality is fast.
4. **Close (10s):** GitHub + demo link. Attention auctions, onchain.
